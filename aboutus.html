From a Kitchen Sink
to Your Doorstep

Founded in 2021, LatherBox was born out of a frustration with the synthetic fragrances and plastic waste found in high-street bath products. Our founder, Sarah, began experimenting with cold-process soap making and soothing bath bombs to heal her own sensitive skin.

Today, we occupy a small carbon-neutral studio, but our mission remains exactly the same: to create products that smell like real nature, feel like pure luxury, and leave zero footprint behind.

Our Core Values

Plant-Powered

We exclusively use food-grade oils, therapeutic essential oils, and organic botanicals. No synthetics, ever.

Earth First

From compostable shipping mailers to biodegradable mica glitters, our packaging is 100% plastic-free.

Small Batch

We don't do mass production. Each bomb is hand-pressed and each soap bar is cut by hand in our UK studio.
