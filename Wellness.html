Creating Your Wellness Ritual

01

Set the Atmosphere

Dim the lights, put your phone on silent, and light a natural candle. Preparation is half the ritual.

02

Temperature Control

Ensure the water is between 37-39°C. This is the optimal temperature for muscle relaxation and sweat detoxification.

03

Intentional Breathing

As the bomb fizzes, practice box breathing. Inhale for 4, hold for 4, exhale for 4. Feel the essential oils work.
